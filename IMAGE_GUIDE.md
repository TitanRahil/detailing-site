## 💡 Pro Tips for Detailing Photography:
1. **Format**: Save photos as `.jpg` or `.webp` at 80–85% quality to keep file sizes under 250 KB for instant 60fps load times.
2. **Lighting**: Low-ambient lighting with strong directional LED light bars (e.g. Scangrip or ceiling hex grids) gives that ultra-high-end atelier aesthetic.
3. **Finish**: Avoid artificial over-saturation. Car detailers prize true paint clarity, tight hydrophobic contact angles, and deep zero-defect gloss.
